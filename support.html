快速开始（简体中文）

  1. 在你的 macOS 或 Windows 电脑上安装并启动 All in One Desktop（或运行 CLI 桥接器），登录同一账号。
  2. 在 iPhone / iPad 上打开 All in One AI 并登录同一账号。
  3. 点击顶部端到端加密（E2EE）图标，扫描电脑端显示的配对二维码完成零知识密钥同步，即可实时查看与操控电脑上的 AI 会话。

常见问题

  • 手机无法直连电脑？ 请确保电脑端 All in One 保持在线；若跨公网处于受限 NAT 环境，应用会自动协商安全的加密中继通道。
  • 提示“需要同步端到端密钥”？ 每台电脑独立生成端到端加密密钥，在新手机上首次连接该电脑时，只需在电脑端展示二维码并用手机扫码一次即可。
  • 如何保存图片或文档？ 在对话中点击图片预览或附件卡片的保存/分享按钮，图片可直接存入系统相册，文档可通过系统分享菜单保存至“文件”应用。
  • 如何永久注销账号？ 打开应用内 设置 → 账号与安全 → 注销账号，验证密码或用户名后即可立即彻底删除账号及所有绑定信息。

Quick Start & FAQ (English)

  1. Install and launch All in One Desktop (or the portable CLI bridge) on your macOS or Windows computer and sign in.
  2. Open All in One AI on your iPhone or iPad and sign in to the same account.
  3. Tap the E2EE shield icon to scan the pairing QR code displayed on your computer and start managing your AI sessions securely.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • My phone cannot connect to my computer: Verify your computer bridge is online. On the same Wi-Fi/LAN, grant Local Network access for fast direct WebRTC P2P; over cellular/remote networks, encrypted TURN fallback is negotiated automatically.
  • Prompted to sync E2EE key: Encryption keys never leave your devices in plaintext. Scan the computer's E2EE QR code once per new mobile device.
  • How do I save images or deliverables? Tap the download/share button on any image or file card to save photos to your Photo Library or export documents via the iOS Share Sheet.
  • How do I delete my account? Go to Settings → Account & Security → Delete Account inside the app to permanently remove your account, linked computers, and active sessions.
