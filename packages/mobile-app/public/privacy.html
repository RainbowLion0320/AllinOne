简体中文

All in One AI（Bundle ID: com.allinone.dshmobile）是一款面向个人开发者的跨设备 AI 远程控制客户端，用于通过端到端加密（E2EE）P2P 通道连接你本人电脑上的本地 AI 工具。我们严格遵守数据最小化原则，不投放广告、不追踪用户、不出售或向第三方共享你的个人数据。

1. 端到端加密与零知识架构

  • 你的所有对话内容、代码片段、终端输出、图片与文件均在你的手机与个人电脑之间通过 AES-256-GCM / WebRTC DTLS 端到端加密传输。
  • 配对密钥仅保存在你自己的设备本地（通过扫描你电脑上的二维码完成配对），中继服务器无法解密你的任何对话内容或本地文件。
  • 本项目支持连接用户自行部署的私有中继服务器。

2. 我们收集的最小化信息

  • 账号标识信息：用户名、加盐哈希后的密码（scrypt）、可选绑定的电子邮箱（仅用于登录二次验证或找回密码）以及可选绑定的 GitHub 账号 ID。
  • 设备连接元数据：已绑定电脑的名称与系统平台、登录会话记录（设备类型与最近登录 IP，用于安全审计和强制下线异常设备）以及中继带宽月度计量统计。
  • 我们不收集：通讯录、精准定位、健康数据、财务信息、跨应用广告标识符（IDFA）或任何第三方数据分析 SDK 遥测。

3. 系统权限说明（iOS / Android）

  • 相机权限：仅在你主动点击扫码配对时调用，用于扫描电脑端生成的端到端加密二维码；不会录像或上传相机画面。
  • 照片图库权限：仅在你主动选择图片发送给电脑端 AI 助手，或主动点击保存对话中的图片到系统相册时使用。
  • 本地网络权限：用于发现并与同一局域网内的个人电脑建立低延迟 WebRTC P2P 直连，减少中继流量消耗。

4. 数据保留与账号注销

你可以随时在应用内永久注销账号：打开 All in One AI → 设置 → 账号与安全 → 注销账号（永久删除），通过身份验证后，你的中继账号、绑定邮箱、GitHub 关联、已绑定电脑凭证与全部活跃会话将被立即永久删除且不可恢复。你本地电脑上的项目文件与对话历史由你本人设备保留，不受影响。

5. 联系我们

如对本隐私政策或数据权利有任何疑问，请访问 技术支持页面 或在 GitHub Issues 提交反馈。

English

All in One AI (Bundle ID: com.allinone.dshmobile) is a cross-device remote control client that connects your mobile device to AI coding tools running on your own personal computer over an end-to-end encrypted (E2EE) peer-to-peer channel. We follow strict data minimization: no ads, no third-party tracking SDKs, and no selling or sharing of personal data.

1. End-to-End Encryption & Zero-Knowledge Design

  • All conversations, source code, tool outputs, images, and deliverables are encrypted end-to-end between your mobile device and your own computer using AES-256-GCM over WebRTC DTLS.
  • Encryption keys remain strictly on your devices (exchanged locally via QR code pairing). The relay server only handles WebRTC signaling and encrypted ciphertext and cannot decrypt your content.
  • You may also point the app to your own self-hosted relay server at any time.

2. Minimal Account Data We Process

  • Account credentials: username, salted scrypt password hash, optional email address (used solely for password recovery and optional 2FA), and optional GitHub OAuth identifier.
  • Connection metadata: names and OS labels of your paired computers, active device session logs (device type and recent login IP for security review and remote sign-out), and monthly relay bandwidth counters.
  • Not collected: contacts, precise location, health/financial data, advertising identifiers (IDFA), or third-party behavioral analytics.

3. Device Permissions

  • Camera: used exclusively when you tap Scan QR to pair E2EE keys with your computer. Camera frames are processed on-device and never uploaded.
  • Photo Library: used only when you explicitly pick an image to send to your computer or save a generated image to Photos.
  • Local Network: used to establish direct low-latency WebRTC peer-to-peer connections with your own computer on the same LAN.

4. Data Retention & In-App Account Deletion

You can permanently delete your account directly inside the app at any time via Settings → Account & Security → Delete Account. Upon confirmation, your relay account, linked email/GitHub association, computer bindings, and all active sessions are immediately and permanently erased. Local files on your own computers are untouched.

5. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, visit our Support Page or open an issue on GitHub.
